**Role:** AI Product Strategy Researcher · **Company:** Logitech · **Timeline:** Jan – Jun 2026

### The question

Where should physical AI take gaming peripherals over the next decade, and which opportunities are both technically feasible and meaningful to players?

### What I did

- **Defined a physical AI strategy** for gaming peripherals as a technically grounded 5–15 year roadmap.
- **Turned emerging capabilities into use cases**: real-time feedback systems, adaptive ergonomics, and biometrics, each translated into prioritized use-case narratives and ROI models.
- **Combined research with user voice**: synthesized a 97-page research report and 17 user interviews, connecting unmet user pain points to differentiated AI-enabled product concepts.
- **Presented to senior leadership** in a 30-slide deliverable that evaluated feasibility constraints alongside opportunity.
