**Result:** 1st Place, PG&E National Home Electrification Challenge · **Field:** 9 undergraduate and MBA finalist teams · **Date:** April 2026, Oakland, CA

### The challenge

How can a utility get more households to make electrification upgrades, and keep them engaged along the way?

### What we built

- **A smart home energy app paired with an ambassador network**, designed as a high-fidelity Figma prototype.
- **A deployment model under $100K**, with projected outcomes for engagement, satisfaction, and upgrade conversion.
