**Role:** Marketing & UI/UX Design Intern · **Organization:** Nuoc Solutions, a Gates Foundation-funded water sustainability nonprofit · **Timeline:** Jun 2023 – Aug 2024

### The problem

The site wasn't turning visitors into advocates. Analytics and conversations with users pointed to friction in how people moved through the site.

### What I did

- **Found 5+ funnel friction points** by pairing site analytics with user interviews, then turned the findings into personas and journey maps.
- **Rebuilt the site in Figma and WordPress** with a responsive layout, clearer visual hierarchy, and accessibility-minded contrast and navigation.
- **Presented a marketing strategy roadmap** to the Board of Directors, which approved it unanimously.
