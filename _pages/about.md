Hi, I'm **Vit** (Nguyen Do). I build products by starting with people: what they need, where they get stuck, and what would make their day easier. I study **Economics and Cognitive Science, with a minor in Data Science, at UC Berkeley** (Class of 2028), and I'm recruiting for **product management, product design, and business roles**.

Right now I'm a **Product Management Intern at [MoMo](https://www.momo.vn/)**, Vietnam's #1 financial super app, where I led the 0-to-1 launch of an AI travel itinerary planner for a travel business serving 30M+ users. Before that, I was an **AI Product Strategy Researcher at Logitech**, building a 5–15 year physical AI roadmap for gaming peripherals that I presented to senior leadership.

I like work at the intersection of research, design, and strategy:

- **Product:** 0-to-1 launches, OKRs, and roadmaps that keep 7+ teams pointed at the same goal
- **Design:** user interviews, personas and journey maps, and high-fidelity Figma prototypes (including one that [won 1st place at PG&E's National Home Electrification Challenge]({{ '/projects/3_pge/' | relative_url }}))
- **Business:** ROI models, go-to-market, and growth, from +78% site traffic to +115% beta sign-ups

Outside of work, I co-founded **Vietbay Teens**, a cultural nonprofit that has raised $115K for Vietnamese nonprofits. I also love sitcoms, sewing, thrifting, crocheting, road trips, Blood Orange, the Cocteau Twins, and anything in teal or sage green (hence this site).
