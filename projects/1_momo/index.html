AI Travel Itinerary Planner

MoMo · 0-to-1 launch of an AI trip planner for a 30M+ user super app

Role: Product Management Intern, Online Travel Assistant · Company: MoMo, Vietnam’s #1 financial platform and super app · Timeline: Dec 2025 – Present

The problem

MoMo’s travel experience spans 5 verticals and serves 30M+ users, but planning a trip still meant a lot of manual searching and booking across separate flows. Users needed one place to go from “I want to travel” to a booked plan.

What I did

  • Led the 0-to-1 launch of an AI-driven travel itinerary planner, taking it from user research through definition and design to a scheduled release.
  • Ran weekly OKR read-outs across 7 teams (design, marketing, engineering, growth, and others) using scrum/agile workflows, keeping product strategy tied to the user needs with the most impact.
  • Built the case for launch and secured senior leadership approval for a summer travel beta and a Lunar New Year campaign.
  • Now building an internal AI tool: a meeting booking and briefing bot aimed at removing ~10 hours/week of workflow inefficiencies.
